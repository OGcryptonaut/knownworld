# Knownworld — product brief for the design pass

Design 2–3 genuinely different directions for this product. You choose the
visual language, type, palette, layout, and which screens best sell each
direction. Nothing visual is prescribed here.

## What it is
Knownworld turns your own Telegram history into a private, researched map of
who you actually know, and finds the warm path to any job, deal or person in
it. Five agents: ingest (runs in your browser), distill (contact rows out of
chats), research + verify (public evidence with citations; verdicts computed
in code, never guessed), job scout (live public job feeds), and
ask-your-network (jobs / people / brief / warm intro, answered from your own
contacts with warm paths instead of cold lists).

## Who it's for
Crypto/web3 professionals whose network lives on Telegram. Constant
job-hopping; "who do I know at X" is a daily question. Their feeds are
dark-neon Telegram, X, trading dashboards — decide how to stand out in that
context.

## The promise (must be unmistakable in every direction)
1. Your export never leaves your browser.
2. We never store a message. Batches go in, distilled rows come out, the batch
   is discarded. Only the rows and research cards you can see, edit and delete
   persist — in your account.
3. Delete everything in one click, or self-deploy into your own Google Cloud
   and trust nobody.

## Business model
Open-core: free open-source self-deploy, plus a paid hosted tier sold on
convenience (no Google Cloud account, no scripts). Pricing comes later; the
site needs a place for it.

## Name
Open. Knownworld is the incumbent, with an existing mark (an open world-ring
with a small constellation inside — "you" as the bright node). Explore
lockups; propose an alternative name if a direction wants one.

## Voice on hand (use, adapt, or replace)
"Your network already knows the way in." · "this is my known world" · "warm
paths instead of cold lists" · "architecture, not promises" · "No guesses."

## Proof on hand
Open source (MIT) · 186 automated tests · 5 agents · 5 live job-feed
integrations · an 878 MB real export handled entirely in-browser · a 4-minute
demo video.

## Surfaces each direction must cover
A public marketing site (landing with the data boundary explained, a privacy
page, room for pricing) AND the product itself in the same skin: onboarding
(upload → distill → research), the contact database (map, network graph,
table, an expanded contact card with evidence), the ask-your-network
conversation, and the privacy/settings screen. Mobile matters.

## Hard constraints
- Invented people only. Companies may be real public companies, but never a
  real person's name or likeness anywhere in a mockup.
- The data boundary must be visibly explained in each direction.
- Directions must differ in kind, not in shade.

Deliverable: one canvas to browse and edit, 2–3 directions, each self-contained
enough to choose from.
