# Step 1 · Export your Telegram data

Only **Telegram Desktop** can export. Download it at https://desktop.telegram.org (macOS/Windows/Linux). The phone and web apps do not have this feature.

1. Open Telegram Desktop and log in.
2. Settings → Advanced → **Export Telegram Data**.
3. Select: Personal chats ✓, Private groups ✓ (optional), **Machine-readable JSON** as format. Deselect media (photos/videos) — not needed and huge.
4. Choose an export folder and start. Large accounts take a while; Telegram may enforce a 24h waiting period on first export for security — that's normal, come back when it unlocks.
5. Point the app's onboarding at the exported folder (it reads `result.json`).

Your export never leaves your machine.
